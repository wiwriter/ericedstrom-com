My post [Top 5 Fantasy Books my readers say got them started](/blog/the-top-5-fantasy-books-my-readers-say-got-them-started/) sure stirred up some controversy.

So here are more books that my readers insist deserve consideration:

***Memory, Sorrow and Thorn Trilogy* by Tad Williams:** (which is 4 books because the last one was so enormously long they had to split it in two). Start with [The Dragon Bone Chair](https://www.amazon.com/Dragonbone-Chair-Memory-Sorrow-Thorn-ebook/dp/B00316UMPS/?tag=wwwericedstro-20).

I read this series a very, very long time ago. But I remember being absolutely enthralled. So if you love epic fantasy (and I mean epic), this series is a must.

![The Dragonbone Chair: Book One of Memory, Sorrow, and Thorn by [Tad Williams]](/images/blog/51P6LxIr9pL.jpg)

[**_Tigana_ by Guy Gavriel Kay**:](https://www.amazon.com/Tigana-Anniversary-Guy-Gavriel-Kay-ebook/dp/B00NZMDA0M/?tag=wwwericedstro-20) I read this back in college, I think. Maybe earlier than that. Kay is a writer’s writer. A number of emails came in reminding me of this one.

![Tigana: Anniversary Edition by [Guy Gavriel Kay]](/images/blog/51P1Mu0sG1L.jpg)

**_[The Mists of Avalon](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B000FC1JCQ?tag=wwwericedstro-20)_ by Marion Zimmer Bradley:** I got my copy through the science fiction and fantasy book club (remember when you could get 10 hardcovers for a buck when you signed up). I didn’t really want to read it, but then I did. Holy cow! The Arthurian legend told from the point of view of the women; and what a powerhouse classic it was. I loaned this book to a girl I knew. Today she is my wife of 23 years.

![The Mists of Avalon: A Novel by [Marion Zimmer Bradley]](/images/blog/41II-qq8gpL.jpg)

**_[The Deed of Paksenarrion](https://www.amazon.com/Deed-Paksenarrion-combo-volumes-Book-ebook/dp/B00APA1E96/?tag=wwwericedstro-20)_ by Elizabeth Moon:** I read this just a year or so ago and I LOVED it! Paks is a sheepherder’s daughter who feels a calling to be something more. Namely a warrior. What I love about this series was Paks’s innate nobility. A hero of honor who it is so easy to root for. The link takes you to an omnibus edition of books 1-3 and it is a steal.

![The Deed of Paksenarrion (Paksenarrion Series combo volumes Book 1) by [Elizabeth Moon]](/images/blog/51J9GCnpMyL.jpg)

**_[Chronicles of Amber](https://www.amazon.com/Nine-Princes-Amber-Book-Chronicles-ebook/dp/B011MYPIY0/?tag=wwwericedstro-20)_ by Roger Zelazny**: this is a long series of relatively short books. People who love Amber LOOOVVE Amber a lot. I think I read only the first couple books in the series, but now I’m adding the whole lot to my list after so many people recommended them.

![Nine Princes in Amber: Book One (The Chronicles of Amber 1) by [Roger Zelazny]](/images/blog/51Vp-z8_POL.jpg)

**_The[ Sword of Truth](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B08KH4G4R8/?tag=wwwericedstro-20)_** _series by Terry Goodkind:_ You have spoken. I have recorded it here. According to my newsletter subscribers any list of important fantasy novels that doesn’t include The Sword of Truth series is useless.

Terry died this past week, so maybe it’s time to check out this enormously expansive classic of the genre. Here’s the first in the series.

![Wizard's First Rule by [Terry Goodkind]](/images/blog/51b8MeaVNOL.jpg)

**_[The Riftwar Saga](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B073TJ3J3J?tag=wwwericedstro-20)_ by Raymond E. Feist:** This is another one of those series I read when I was 14 or 15 and never went back to. Now I’m afraid to re-read it because I loved it so much. I only got a few emails about this series, but I’m including it because those who mentioned it tended to put it near their all-time favorites list.

![Magician: Apprentice (Riftwar Cycle: The Riftwar Saga Book 1) by [Raymond E. Feist]](/images/blog/51v6u24fn5L.jpg)
